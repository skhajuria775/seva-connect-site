Madadgar Kaise Bane — Poora Tareeka

8 min padhne mein · Shuruwaat Karein

Madadgar banna matlab hai — apni marzi se, apne budget mein, kisi genuine zarooratmand ki madad karna. Jitna aur jab bhi aap kar sakte hain, utna hi kaafi hai.

Registration form kahan hai

Form is page par hai. Yahan aap batate hain ki aap kis tarah ki madad karna chahte hain, aur kitni.

Aap kis category mein madad kar sakte hain

Aap ek ya ek se zyada category chun sakte hain:

  • 🍚 Khaana
  • 📚 Padhaai
  • 🏥 Ilaaj
  • 🏠 Aashiyana
  • 👕 Kapde
  • 💼 Hunar/Naukri
  • 👴 Buzurg Seva
  • 🚨 Emergency

Budget — aap kitni madad kar sakte hain

Aapko apna budget batana hota hai — yeh woh rakam hai jo aap madad ke liye de sakte hain. Yeh matching mein bahut zaroori hai: system sirf woh case aapko dikhayega jinki zaroorat aapke budget ke andar aati ho.

Kitni door tak madad karna chahte hain

Ek aur zaroori sawaal hai — "Aap Kahan Tak Help Kar Sakte Hain?" Iske 4 options hain:

  • 🏙️ Sirf meri city mein
  • 🗺️ Mere state mein
  • 📍 Poore India mein
  • 🌍 Kaheen bhi (Sabse zyada help hogi)

Yeh option decide karta hai ki jab aapke shehar/rajya mein koi fit case na mile, tab kya aapko duur ke case bhi dikhaye jaayen. Jitni zyada door tak aap taiyar hain, utne zyada log aap tak pahunch sakte hain.

Kitni baar madad karna chahte hain

Aap yeh bhi bata sakte hain ki aap sirf ek baar madad karna chahte hain, ya niyamit roop se (monthly). Yeh aapki suvidha ke hisaab se hai.

Registration ke baad status

Naya Madadgar default roop se ACTIVE status mein hota hai. Iska matlab hai ki matching turant aapko eligible maan sakta hai, agar koi fit case ho.

Jab aapko match milta hai

Agar koi case aapki category, budget aur reach se match karta hai, aapko email jaata hai. Aap khud faisla lete hain ki madad karni hai ya nahi — koi zabardasti nahi hoti.

Active se Inactive kaise hota hai

Agar aap lagatar 2 notifications par jawab nahi dete, toh system aapko khud-b-khud INACTIVE kar deta hai. Isse matching sirf un Madadgars ko dikhayi jaati hai jo abhi turant available hain, taaki Zaruratmand ka case atak na jaaye.

Ghabraiye mat: Inactive hona koi block (ban) nahi hai. Aap jab bhi tayyar ho, dobara active ho sakte hain.

Agar aapne "sirf ek baar" wala option chuna tha, toh madad poori hone ke baad aapka status khud-b-khud inactive ho jaata hai — taaki aapko baar-baar notification na milein.

Aapka pehla match milne ke baad

Jab aap kisi case ke liye "Maine Madad Kar Di" confirm karte hain, ek poora process shuru hota hai — deadline, Shukriya video, aur reward. Woh sab agle article mein.
