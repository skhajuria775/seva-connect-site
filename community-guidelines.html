🤝 Community Guidelines

Seva Connect ek insaani rishta hai — aur har rishte ko respect, honesty, aur kindness ki zarurat hoti hai. Yeh hamari community ke basic rules hain.

Sabke Liye — Basic Principles

Chahe aap Madadgar ho, Zaruratmand ho, ya Seva Saathi — yeh rules sabke liye hain.

🙏

Respect Se Baat Karo

Har insaan ki situation alag hoti hai. Judgment ya sharam dilana kabhi theek nahi hai.

✅

Sach Bolo

Apni story, apni zarurat, apni identity — sab sahi aur honest hona chahiye. Galat information se kisi ki jagah ki madad chhin jaati hai.

🔒

Privacy Ka Khayal Rakho

Kisi ki phone number, address, ya personal details ko platform ke bahar share mat karo bina permission ke.

💬

Seedha Aur Saaf Communication

Confusion se bachne ke liye clear baat karo — kya madad chahiye, kya de sakte ho, kab tak.

Madadgar Ke Liye

❤️

Bina Expectation Madad Karo

Madad dene ka matlab koi obligation create nahi karta. Zaruratmand par koi pressure nahi daalna.

🚫

Koi Inappropriate Demand Nahi

Madad ke badle koi personal favor, photo, ya kisi tarah ka exchange maangna sakht mana hai.

📞

Direct Contact Responsibly Use Karo

Zaruratmand ka number sirf madad coordinate karne ke liye hai — kisi aur purpose ke liye nahi.

Zaruratmand Ke Liye

📝

Sahi Information Do

Apni zarurat, amount, aur situation ko honestly describe karo. Yeh verification mein bhi help karta hai.

🙏

Shukriya Video Zaroor Bhejo

Jab help mil jaaye, ek chhota thank-you video Madadgar ke liye bahut matlab rakhta hai — aur platform ki trust banata hai.

Seva Saathi Ke Liye

📣

Sirf Genuine Logo Ko Encourage Karo

Madadgar laana ka matlab quantity nahi, quality hai — wahi log laao jo sach mein madad karna chahte hain.

🌱

Mission Ko Sahi Tareeke Se Represent Karo

Jab aap platform share karo, hamesha emotional aur honest tareeke se baat karo — kabhi exaggerate ya misleading claims nahi.

🚫 Yeh Kabhi Allowed Nahi Hai

  • Fake cases, fake stories, ya fake documents submit karna
  • Kisi ko harass, threaten, ya discriminate karna
  • Madad ke badle paisa maangna ya commission lena
  • Platform ka use scam ya fraud ke liye karna
  • Kisi ki personal information ko bina permission ke share karna
  • Spam ya irrelevant content post karna

Agar koi in rules ko break karta hai, hum unka account suspend kar sakte hain aur zaroorat padne par authorities ko bhi inform kar sakte hain.

Kuch Report Karna Hai?

Agar aapko koi behavior guidelines ke against lagta hai, hamein turant bataye.
