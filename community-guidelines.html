🤝 Community Guidelines

Seva Connect ek insaani rishta hai — aur har rishte ki buniyad respect, honesty, kindness aur trust hai. Yeh Community Guidelines hamari community ke basic rules hain, taaki Madadgar, Zarooratmand aur Seva Saathi ek doosre ke saath dignity, honesty aur safety ke saath jud saken.

Sabke Liye — Basic Community Rules

Chahe aap Madadgar ho, Zarooratmand ho, ya Seva Saathi — yeh Community Guidelines sabke liye hain. Har interaction mein respect, honesty, privacy aur safety ka khayal rakhein.

🙏

Respect Se Baat Karo

Har insaan ki situation alag hoti hai. Kisi ko judge, shame, insult ya neecha dikhana theek nahi hai. Har vyakti ke saath dignity aur kindness se baat karein.

✅

Sach Bolo

Apni story, apni zarurat, apni identity — sab sahi aur honest hona chahiye. Galat information se kisi ki jagah ki madad chhin jaati hai.

🔒

Privacy Ka Khayal Rakho

Kisi ka phone number, address ya personal information bina permission ke platform ke bahar share mat karo. Zarooratmand ki personal details ko sirf genuine madad coordinate karne ke purpose se use karein.

💬

Seedha Aur Saaf Communication

Confusion se bachne ke liye clear baat karo — kya madad chahiye, kya de sakte ho, kab tak.

Madadgar Ke Liye

❤️

Bina Expectation Madad Karo

Madad karna kisi Zarooratmand par personal obligation ya pressure create nahi karta. Madad hamesha respect aur bina kisi inappropriate expectation ke karein.

🚫

Koi Inappropriate Demand Nahi

Madad ke badle personal favor, private photo, personal information ya kisi bhi tarah ka inappropriate exchange maangna sakht mana hai.

📞

Direct Contact Responsibly Use Karo

Zaruratmand ka number sirf madad coordinate karne ke liye hai — kisi aur purpose ke liye nahi.

Zaruratmand Ke Liye

📝

Sahi Information Do

Apni zarurat, amount aur situation ko honestly describe karo. Sahi information community mein trust banane aur case verification process mein madad karti hai.

🙏

Shukriya Video Zaroor Bhejo

Jab help mil jaaye, ek chhota thank-you video Madadgar ke liye bahut matlab rakhta hai — aur platform ki trust banata hai.

Seva Saathi Ke Liye

📣

Sirf Genuine Logo Ko Encourage Karo

Madadgar laana quantity ka nahi, genuine intent ka matter hai — wahi log encourage karein jo sach mein kisi ki madad karna chahte hain.

🌱

Mission Ko Sahi Tareeke Se Represent Karo

Jab aap platform share karo, hamesha emotional aur honest tareeke se baat karo — kabhi exaggerate ya misleading claims nahi.

🚫 Yeh Kabhi Allowed Nahi Hai

  • Fake cases, fake stories, ya fake documents submit karna
  • Kisi ko harass, threaten, ya discriminate karna
  • Madad ke badle paisa maangna ya commission lena
  • Platform ka use scam ya fraud ke liye karna
  • Kisi ki personal information ko bina permission ke share karna
  • Spam ya irrelevant content post karna

Agar koi in rules ko break karta hai, hum unka account suspend kar sakte hain aur zaroorat padne par authorities ko bhi inform kar sakte hain.

🛡️ Dispute Resolution & Accountability

Trust Seva Connect ki neenv hai. Isliye connections mein transparency aur dono-taraf accountability important hai. Verification process ka purpose yeh hai ki agar confirmation aur actual situation mein koi mismatch ho, toh usse review kiya ja sake.

Yeh Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

1

Jab Madadgar "Maine Madad Kar Di" confirm karta hai, Zaruratmand se bhi seedha verify kiya jaata hai ki madad sach mein mili ya nahi.

2

Agar koi mismatch ho — matlab confirmation aur reality mein farak ho — dusre insaan se ek tay samay ke andar proof manga jaata hai.

3

Hamari team har proof ko manually verify karti hai, kisi automatic ya jaldi-baazi wale decision se nahi.

4

Jo galat information deta hua paya jaata hai (chahe Madadgar ho ya Zaruratmand), uska account permanently block kar diya jaata hai.

5

Aur agar kisi genuine wajah se koi deadline miss ho jaaye, hamari team case-by-case manually review karti hai — sirf automatic timer par decision nahi lete.

Yehi wajah hai ki Seva Connect par har connection — chahe madad dene wala ho ya lene wala — dono taraf se accountable rehta hai.

Kuch Report Karna Hai?

Agar aapko koi behaviour in Community Guidelines ke against lagta hai, hamein bataye. Safe aur respectful community banaye rakhne mein aapki awaaz important hai.
