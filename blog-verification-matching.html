Verification Aur Matching Kaise Hoti Hai

10 min padhne mein · Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Registration ke baad har case ek insaan dekhta hai — koi bhi case apne aap pass nahi hota. Verification ke turant baad shuru hoti hai matching — jisse sabse sahi Madadgar dhoondha jaata hai.

Admin verification — ek insaan ki nazar

Har naya case, register hone ke baad, admin ke saamne "Verify Karo" button ke saath aata hai. Yeh jaan-boojh kar aisa rakha gaya hai — taaki har case ko koi insaan dekhe, sirf computer nahi. Jab tak admin case ko verify nahi karta, matching shuru nahi hoti.

Matching kaise shuru hoti hai

Jaise hi admin verify karta hai, system turant sabse kareeb ka Madadgar dhoondhna shuru karta hai. Yeh dhoondhna ek tarteeb (order) mein hota hai:

  1. Pincode — sabse pehle bilkul aas-paas ke Madadgar dekhe jaate hain, wahi pincode wale.
  2. Shehar (City) — agar pincode mein koi na mile, toh poore shehar mein dekha jaata hai.
  3. Rajya (State) — agar shehar mein bhi na mile, toh poore rajya mein.
  4. Poora India — agar rajya mein bhi na mile, toh poore desh mein.
  5. Global — aur agar phir bhi na mile, toh bina kisi jagah ki seema ke.

Jaise hi kisi ek level par eligible Madadgar mil jaate hain, dhoondhna wahin ruk jaata hai. Isse match hamesha jitna ho sake utna kareeb ka milta hai.

Kaunsa Madadgar eligible mana jaata hai

Har level par, sirf woh Madadgar dekhe jaate hain jo teen sharton ko poora karte hain:

  • Woh Madadgar ki category, Zaruratmand ki zaroorat ki category se match karti ho.
  • Madadgar ka budget, Zaruratmand ki zaroorat ki rakam se zyada ya barabar ho.
  • Madadgar location ke hisaab se us level par fit baithta ho (jaise pincode-level par same pincode).

Top-3 Madadgar — ek primary, do standby

Jo Madadgar eligible milte hain, unme se sabse behtar 3 chune jaate hain — sabse pehle jinhone sabse zyada log ki madad ki hai unhe priority milti hai, aur agar madad ki sankhya barabar ho, toh jiska budget zaroorat ke sabse kareeb ho use priority milti hai.

In 3 mein se sirf pehle Madadgar ko turant email jaata hai. Baaki 2 standby mein rakhe jaate hain — unhe abhi email nahi jaata.

48 ghante ka intezaar — agar pehla Madadgar jawab na de

Agar pehla Madadgar 48 ghante tak koi jawab nahi deta, system khud-b-khud agle standby Madadgar ko email bhej deta hai — taaki Zaruratmand ka case kabhi ruk na jaaye.

Iske liye ek daily check chalta hai jo dekhta hai ki kaunse case 48 ghante se zyada intezaar kar chuke hain, aur unke liye agle Madadgar ko notify kar deta hai.

Agar kisi bhi level par Madadgar na mile

Agar poore India ya Global level mein bhi koi eligible Madadgar nahi milta, toh case ka status WAITLIST ho jaata hai. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki case reject ho gaya — jaise hi koi naya Madadgar register karta hai jo fit baithta hai, purane waitlist wale cases dobara check kiye jaate hain.

Agla step

Jab Madadgar ko notification milta hai aur woh madad karne ka faisla karta hai, agla kadam hai — confirmation aur Shukriya. Woh poora process agle article mein.
