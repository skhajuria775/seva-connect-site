🔍 Verification Policy

Har case jo Seva Connect par dikhta hai, woh hamari team dwara manually verify kiya gaya hota hai. Yahan janiye hum yeh kaise karte hain.

Verification Itni Zaroori Kyun Hai?

Seva Connect par koi bhi Madadgar apna paisa, samaan, ya samay seedha kisi Zaruratmand ko deta hai — bina kisi beech ke. Iska matlab hai ki trust sabse important cheez hai. Hum yeh sunishchit karte hain ki har case genuine ho, taaki aapki madad sahi jagah pahunche.

Jab tak ek case "VERIFIED" status nahi le leta, woh kabhi bhi public website par nahi dikhaya jaata.

Hamari Verification Process

1

Zaruratmand Apni Story Submit Karta Hai

Naam, phone, city, category, aur kam se kam 30 shabdon ki story ke saath registration form bharte hain.

2

Video Ya Document Proof

Zarurat ka short video (ya supporting documents — jaise medical reports, school fee receipts) submit karna zaroori hota hai.

3

Admin Manual Review

Hamari team khud video dekhti hai, documents check karti hai, aur jaroorat pade to phone par seedha baat karti hai — yeh sab 24 ghante ke andar hota hai.

4

VERIFIED Status

Jab admin santusht ho jaata hai, case ko "VERIFIED" mark kiya jaata hai — sirf tab woh Cases page par live hota hai aur Madadgar se match hone ke liye eligible banta hai.

Hum Kya Check Karte Hain

✅Story consistent hai documents/video ke saath
✅Phone number active aur verifiable hai
✅Amount needed realistic aur justified hai
✅Koi duplicate ya repeated submission nahi hai
✅Location details sahi hain (city, pincode)
✅Category genuine zarurat se match karti hai

Fraud Zero Tolerance: Agar kisi case mein galat information mile — chahe verification ke pehle ya baad mein — us case ko turant hata diya jaata hai aur future submissions block kar diye jaate hain.

Help Milne Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai?

Jab Madadgar Zaruratmand ki madad karta hai, Zaruratmand ek chhoti Shukriya video banata hai. Yeh video bhi admin verify karta hai before Stories page par dikhane ke. Isse double-check ho jaata hai ki help genuinely pahunchi.

Suspicious Case Dekha?

Agar aapko koi case suspicious lagta hai, ya aap kisi case ki authenticity ke baare mein doubt rakhte hain, hum chahte hain aap turant hamein bataye.
