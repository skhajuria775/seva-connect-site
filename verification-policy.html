🔍 Seva Connect Verification Policy

Seva Connect par public hone wale cases ko hamari team manual verification process ke through review karti hai. Yahan jaaniye ki case verification kaise hoti hai, kya information check ki jaati hai aur VERIFIED status kab diya jaata hai.

Verification Itni Zaroori Kyun Hai?

Seva Connect par Madadgar apna paisa, samaan ya samay seedha Zaruratmand ko deta hai — bina kisi beech ke. Isliye trust aur case verification platform ke important parts hain.

Seva Connect har submitted case ko review aur verify karne ki process follow karta hai. Jab tak case "VERIFIED" status nahi leta, woh public website par display nahi kiya jaata.

Seva Connect Ki Verification Process

1

Zaruratmand Apni Story Submit Karta Hai

Zaruratmand registration form mein naam, phone, city, category aur kam se kam 80 shabdon ki story submit karta hai. Yeh information verification process ka starting point hoti hai.

2

Video Ya Document Proof

Zarurat ko support karne ke liye short video ya relevant documents — jaise medical reports ya school fee receipts — submit karna zaroori hota hai.

3

Admin Manual Review

Seva Connect team submitted video dekhti hai, documents check karti hai aur zaroorat padne par phone par direct baat karti hai. Current process ke according yeh review 24 se 48 ghante ke andar kiya jaata hai.

4

VERIFIED Status

Jab admin review complete karne ke baad case verification requirements meet karta hai, case ko "VERIFIED" status diya jaata hai. Iske baad hi woh Cases page par public hota hai aur Madadgar ke saath matching ke liye eligible banta hai.

Hum Kya Check Karte Hain

✅Story consistent hai documents/video ke saath
✅Phone number active aur verifiable hai
✅Amount needed realistic aur justified hai
✅Koi duplicate ya repeated submission nahi hai
✅Location details sahi hain (city, pincode)
✅Category genuine zarurat se match karti hai

Galat Information Ke Khilaf Action: Agar kisi case mein verification ke pehle ya baad galat information milti hai, to case ko remove kiya ja sakta hai aur future submissions ko block kiya ja sakta hai.

Help Milne Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai?

Jab Madadgar Zaruratmand ki madad karta hai, Zaruratmand ek chhoti Shukriya video bana sakta hai. Stories page par dikhane se pehle is video ko bhi admin review karta hai. Yeh additional review help ke baad available information ko dobara check karne mein madad karta hai.

Suspicious Case Dekha?

Agar aapko koi case suspicious lagta hai ya uski authenticity par doubt hai, to humein turant inform karein. Seva Connect team available information ke basis par case ko dobara review kar sakti hai.
