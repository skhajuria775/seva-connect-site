Seva Saathi Kaise Bane — Poora Tareeka

9 min padhne mein · Community & Pehchaan

Har Madadgar jo platform tak pahunchta hai, uske peeche kabhi na kabhi koi ek insaan hota hai jisne unhe bataya. Seva Saathi wahi log hain — jo apne aas-paas Seva Connect ka daayra badhate hain.

Seva Saathi kaun ban sakta hai

Koi bhi jo apne mohalle, samaj, ya jaan-pehchaan mein logon ko Madadgar banne ke liye kehna chahta hai, Seva Saathi ban sakta hai. Registration is page par hai.

Kaise kaam karta hai

Registration ke baad aapko ek referral-code milta hai. Jab koi naya Madadgar aapke code se register karta hai, woh aapke saath jud jaata hai. Jaise-jaise aapke laaye hue Madadgar active roop se madad karte hain, aapka level badhta jaata hai.

7 levels — ek-ek karke

Yeh level aapke laaye hue active Madadgars ki ginti se tay hota hai:

  • 🌱 Umeed Ki Kiran — shuru se
  • 🌿 Madad Ka Haath — 1 active Madadgar laane par
  • 🌳 Seva Saathi — 3 active Madadgar laane par
  • ⭐ Dil Ka Diya — 5 active Madadgar laane par
  • 🌟 Insaniyat Ka Sitara — 10 active Madadgar laane par
  • 👑 Umeed Ka Ujala — 25 active Madadgar laane par
  • 🌍 Manav Seva Ratna — 50 active Madadgar laane par

Jaise-jaise aap upar badhte hain, aapki pehchaan Wall of Fame par aur zyada nazar aati hai — top Seva Saathi ki list mein.

Yeh safar ek saada baat batata hai: jitne zyada log genuinely madad karne ke liye aage aate hain, utni hi zyada zindagiyan badalti hain.

Ek zaroori baat

Seva Saathi ka kaam sirf logon ko jodne ka hai — jaise poore platform mein, yahan bhi koi paisa aapke haath se nahi guzarta. Aapka kaam hai logon ko Madadgar banne ke liye kehna; baaki poora process (verification, matching, madad) waisa hi chalta hai jaisa doosre articles mein bataya gaya hai.

Ban ka koi system nahi

Kyunki Seva Saathi dispute wale hisse mein seedhe shaamil nahi hote (woh sirf logon ko jodte hain, madad khud nahi dete), unke liye koi block/ban ka system nahi hai.
