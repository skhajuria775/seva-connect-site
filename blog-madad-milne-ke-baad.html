Madad Milne Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai

9 min padhne mein · Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Jab ek Madadgar madad karne ka faisla karta hai, wahan se lekar case poora (complete) hone tak, har step ka ek uddeshya hai — dono taraf ke logon ka bharosa banaye rakhna.

"Maine Madad Kar Di" — Madadgar ka confirmation

Jab Madadgar Zaruratmand ki madad kar deta hai, woh "Maine Madad Kar Di" button click karta hai. Isi waqt se ek 7-din ki deadline shuru ho jaati hai.

Zaruratmand ko turant ek email jaata hai jisme do button hote hain:

  • Ek Shukriya button — agar sach mein madad mil gayi hai.
  • Ek "Nahi Mili" button — agar madad nahi mili (iske baare mein poora article aage hai).

Shukriya video — asli kahani

Zaruratmand ek chhota video ya sandesh submit karta hai jisme woh Madadgar ka shukriya ada karta hai. Yeh sirf ek formality nahi hai — yehi woh pal hai jo poore platform ko sach mein khaas banata hai. Yeh video baad mein Wall of Fame aur Gallery mein bhi dikhaya ja sakta hai.

Admin approval

Shukriya video submit hone ke baad, admin ek baar aur dekhta hai aur "Approve Karo" click karta hai. Approval hote hi:

  1. Case ka status COMPLETED ho jaata hai.
  2. Madadgar ka help-count (yaani unhone kitni baar madad ki hai) ek se badh jaata hai.
  3. Agar isse Madadgar ka naya badge-level ban gaya ho, toh unhe ek appreciation certificate bhi milta hai.

Madadgar ke badge-levels

Har Madadgar ne kitni baar madad ki hai, uske hisaab se ek badge milta hai:

  • 🌱 Umeed Ki Kiran — shuru se
  • 🌿 Seva Helper — 3 baar madad karne par
  • 🌳 Seva Warrior — 5 baar madad karne par
  • 🌟 Seva Star — 10 baar madad karne par
  • ⭐ Seva Legend — 25 baar madad karne par
  • 🌍 Manav Seva Ratna — 50 baar madad karne par

Yeh badge Wall of Fame par bhi dikhta hai, jahan sabse zyada madad karne wale Madadgar top par nazar aate hain.

Agar Zaruratmand chup rahe — silence ka rasta

Kabhi-kabhi Zaruratmand na Shukriya bhejta hai, na "Nahi Mili" bolta hai. Iske liye bhi ek fair tareeka bana hai:

  • 5-vein din: Ek reminder email jaata hai — "kya madad mil gayi? Bataiye."
  • 7-vein din: Agar phir bhi koi jawab nahi aata, system Madadgar ke confirmation par bharosa karke case ko khud COMPLETED kar deta hai, aur reward Madadgar ko mil jaata hai.

Yeh isliye hai kyunki jis Madadgar ne apna waqt aur mehnat lagayi, uska reward sirf isliye nahi rukna chahiye kyunki Zaruratmand ne jawab nahi diya.

Agar kuch missing ho, ya kuch galat ho

Do aur situations bhi ho sakti hain — agar document ya video poori tarah spasht nahi hai (agla article), ya agar madad sach mein nahi mili (uske baad wala article). Dono ke apne fair tareeke hain.
