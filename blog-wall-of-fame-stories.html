Wall of Fame Aur Shukriya Stories

7 min padhne mein · Community & Pehchaan

Har case ke peeche ek asli kahani hoti hai. Seva Connect par yeh kahaniyan chhupti nahi — woh sabke saamne, poori izzat ke saath, dikhayi jaati hain.

Shukriya videos — jahan se sab shuru hota hai

Jab koi Zaruratmand apna Shukriya video daalta hai (jaisa humne pehle dekha), aur admin usse approve karta hai, woh video sirf Madadgar tak nahi rehta — woh Stories page aur Gallery par bhi dikh sakta hai.

Gallery aur Share-a-Moment

Iske alawa, koi bhi apna ek pal — ek photo, ek anubhav — Share-a-Moment page se Gallery mein daal sakta hai. Yeh community ke liye ek jagah hai jahan poora asar ek saath dikh sake.

Certificates

Jab koi Madadgar ek naya badge-level paar karta hai, unhe ek appreciation certificate milta hai — unke naam aur unke help-count ke saath.

Top Madadgar aur Top Seva Saathi

Platform par un logon ki ek list bhi hai jinhone sabse zyada madad ki hai, aur jin Seva Saathis ne sabse zyada Madadgar joda hai. Yeh sirf ek number nahi hai — yeh un logon ki pehchaan hai jo baar-baar aage aate hain.

Wall of Fame ka uddeshya seedha hai: jo log chup-chaap madad karte hain, unki mehnat ko chupne nahi diya jaata.
