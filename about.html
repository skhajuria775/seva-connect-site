A Message from the Founder

Namaste! Ek aisi muhim mein aapka swagat hai, jo zindagiyan badal rahi hai 🙏

Main Sanjeev Khajuria, Founder – Seva Connect. Yeh platform sirf ek idea nahi, balki ek aisi zaroorat se paida hua hai jise maine kareeb se mehsoos kiya. Maine dekha hai ki samaj mein madad karne ka jazba toh bahut hai, par logo ko ek bharosemand raasta nahi milta. Wahi doosri taraf, zarooratmand log sahi madad ko taraste reh jaate hain. Seva Connect isi khaai ko bharne ka ek majboot sankalp (resolution) hai. Hamara maksad bilkul saaf hai: Aapki madad, seedhe zarooratmand ke haathon mein, bina kisi bicholiye (middleman) ke.

Lekin ek bada badlaav akele nahi laya jaa sakta. Har Madadgar, har Seva Saathi aur har wo insaan jo is muhim (mission) se judta hai, wahi iski asli taaqat hai. Aaiye, haath milayein aur kisi ki zindagi mein umeed ki nayi kiran jagayein.

— Sanjeev Khajuria, Founder & Visionary

Hamari Kahani

Seva Connect Kaise Shuru Hua

Humne dekha ki ek taraf hain log jo genuinely help karna chahte hain — paisa hai, dil hai, time hai — lekin sahi insaan nahi milta jisko help karein.

Doosri taraf hain log jinhe sachchi madad chahiye — lekin untak koi pahunchta nahi. Ya jo pahunchta hai paisa wo bi pura nahi. Paise ka hissab nahi milta. Trust nahi banta.

Yahi gap dekh ke Seva Connect bana — ek aisa platform jahan sirf insaniyat hai. Koi transaction nahi, koi commission nahi. Sirf ek sachcha rishta.

Humne decide kiya ki yeh platform hamesha free rahega — Madadgars ke liye bhi, zarooratmand ke liye bhi. Kyunki zaroorat mein insaanon ki madad karna koi business nahi hai — yeh farz hai.

Aaj Seva Connect India ke kai shehron mein kaam kar raha hai. Har verified case seedha Madadgar tak pahunchta hai. Har shukriya video ek sachchi kahani batata hai. Aur yeh safar ruka nahi hai.
