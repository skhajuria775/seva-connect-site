Milaap, Ketto Aur Seva Connect Mein Kya Farak Hai?

Platform Comparison

Jab madad chahiye hoti hai, to sabse pehla sawal yehi aata hai — "kaunsa platform istemal karun?" Milaap, Ketto, ImpactGuru jaise naam kaafi suna hoga, aur ab Seva Connect bhi ek option hai. Lekin yeh sab ek jaisa kaam nahi karte. Is lekh mein hum dono tarah ke platforms ka model factually samjhaenge — taaki aap apni zarurat ke hisaab se sahi faisla le sakein.

Milaap, Ketto Aur Iss Tarah Ke Platforms Kaise Kaam Karte Hain

Yeh sab crowdfunding platforms hain — matlab inka kaam hai ek campaign banana, use logon tak (donors tak) pahunchana, aur jo bhi paisa collect hota hai, wo pehle platform ke paas aata hai aur phir campaigner ko transfer hota hai. Yeh ek proven aur bada hua model hai — Milaap 2010 se, Ketto 2012 se is field mein hai, aur dono ke paas lakhon donors ka established network hai.

Inka platform-fee zyada tar 0% hota hai — matlab campaign banane ke liye koi fixed charge nahi lagta. Lekin jab bhi koi donor payment karta hai (card, UPI, netbanking se), us transaction ko process karne ke liye ek payment gateway charge lagta hai — yeh charge dono platforms khud apni website par mention karte hain, aur yeh normal hai kyunki koi bhi online payment-processing company (bank ho ya gateway) apni service ka charge leti hai. Iske alawa, kuch premium features (jaise zyada visibility, dedicated support) alag se optional charge par milte hain.

Seva Connect Ka Model Alag Hai — Kaise?

Seva Connect crowdfunding platform nahi hai. Yahan koi bhi paisa platform ke paas se guzarta hi nahi. Jab ek Zaruratmand (jisko madad chahiye) register karta hai aur verify hota hai, to seedha ek Madadgar (jo madad karna chahta hai) unse connect ho jaata hai — aur madad seedhe, bina kisi middleman ke, pahunchti hai. Isiliye Seva Connect na to donation collect karta hai, na hi payment-gateway involve hota hai — kyunki paisa yahan se guzarta hi nahi.

Yehi wajah hai ki Seva Connect har stage par bilkul free rehta hai — na registration ka charge, na verification ka, na madad milne ka.

Fees Ka Honest Comparison

Milaap / Ketto jaise platforms: Platform-fee 0% hoti hai, lekin payment-gateway charge (aam taur par 2-3% ke aas-paas, exact rate platform aur payment-method par nirbhar karta hai) donation par lagta hai, kyunki paisa online transfer hota hai. Yeh koi chhupi hui baat nahi hai — dono platforms apni website par yeh clearly likhte hain.

Seva Connect: Kyunki koi paisa platform se nahi guzarta, isliye koi gateway-charge lagne ka sawaal hi nahi uthta. Poora process 100% free hai.

Yeh farak "kaun behtar hai" ka nahi hai — yeh do alag tarah ke models hain, aur dono ka apna zaroori role hai, jo aage samjhate hain.

Kis Situation Mein Kaunsa Platform Sahi Hai

Yahan hum bilkul honest rahenge, kyunki asli madad ka matlab hai sahi jagah bhejna, chahe woh humara platform ho ya na ho.

Bade fund-raising ke liye (jaise mehenge operations, transplants, lakhon rupaye ki zarurat): Established crowdfunding platforms jaise Milaap ya Ketto zyada suited ho sakte hain, kyunki unke paas saalon ka track record, lakhon active donors, aur bada marketing-reach hai. Ek naye ya chhote platform ke liye itna bada donor-base khada karna time leta hai.

Direct, jaldi, ya chhoti-medium madad ke liye (ration, fees, chhoti medical zarurat, samaan, ya koi bhi cheez jahan ek Madadgar seedha connect ho sake): Seva Connect ka direct-connect model faster aur simpler ho sakta hai, kyunki beech mein koi fund-collection process nahi hai.

Tax-deduction receipt (80G) chahiye: Abhi Seva Connect registered NGO nahi hai, isliye 80G certificate nahi de sakta — Milaap/Ketto par kuch registered NGO campaigns yeh de sakte hain. Yeh ek genuine limitation hai jo hum chhupate nahi hain.

Ek Honest Baat Seva Connect Ke Baare Mein

Seva Connect abhi ek naya platform hai. Humare paas Milaap-Ketto jitna bada track record ya donor-network nahi hai — abhi hum apni pehli genuine cases build kar rahe hain. Jo baat hum confidently keh sakte hain woh yeh hai: humara model shuru se hi zero-commission, direct-connect ke around design hua hai, aur har case verify hota hai registration ke baad. Baaki sab — reach, scale, track record — yeh time ke saath banega, aur hum ismein transparent rehna chahte hain.

Kuch Zaroori Sawal

Kya Milaap aur Ketto sach mein 0% fee lete hain?

Platform-fee 0% hoti hai, lekin payment gateway charges (jab donor card/UPI se paisa deta hai) alag se lagte hain — yeh dono platforms khud apni website par batate hain. Kuch premium features par 5-8% tak ka alag charge bhi ho sakta hai.

Seva Connect kis tarah alag hai Milaap-Ketto se?

Milaap-Ketto par paisa pehle platform ke paas aata hai, phir campaigner ko transfer hota hai. Seva Connect par koi paisa platform ke paas se guzarta hi nahi — Madadgar seedha Zaruratmand ki madad karta hai.

Bada fund raise karna ho to kaunsa platform behtar hai?

Agar lakhon rupaye ek bade donor-network se collect karne hain (jaise bade medical operations), to established crowdfunding platforms zyada suited hain kyunki unka reach aur track record bahut bada hai.

Kya Seva Connect NGO hai ya 80G certificate deta hai?

Nahi, abhi Seva Connect ek registered NGO nahi hai, isliye 80G tax-deduction certificate nahi deta. Yeh ek direct person-to-person connecting platform hai, donation-collection platform nahi.

Antim Baat

Koi bhi ek platform har situation ke liye perfect nahi hota. Bada fund chahiye to established crowdfunding platforms ka apna zaroori role hai. Direct aur fast madad chahiye, bina kisi gateway-charge ke, to Seva Connect ek option hai. Sahi faisla apni zarurat samajh kar lena chahiye — aur hum chahte hain ki aap woh faisla poori jaankari ke saath lein.
