Agar Madad Nahi Mili — Dispute Kaise Karein

10 min padhne mein · Trust & Safety

Bharosa hi is poore system ki neev hai. Yeh article Zaruratmand aur Madadgar, dono ke liye, step-by-step batata hai ki agar madad ko lekar koi vivaad ho jaaye toh kya karna hai.

Jab Bharosa Toota Hua Lage

Yeh bahut kam hota hai, lekin agar kabhi Madadgar ke "Maine Madad Kar Di" confirm karne ke baad bhi Zaruratmand tak sach mein madad na pahunche, toh yeh ek gambhir sthiti ban jaati hai. Is article mein hum poora process, dono taraf se, step-by-step samjhayenge — taaki chahe aap Zaruratmand ho ya Madadgar, aapko apna solution isi ek page par mil jaaye.

Agar Aap Zaruratmand Hain — Step By Step

Step 1: "Nahi Mili" Button Click Karein

Jab Madadgar "Maine Madad Kar Di" confirm karta hai, aapke email mein "Nahi Mili" button hota hai. Agar sach mein madad nahi mili, isse click karein. Click hote hi case ka status Disputed ho jaata hai, aur Madadgar ko turant suchna chali jaati hai.

Zaroori chetavani: Yeh button sirf tab click karein jab sach mein madad nahi mili ho. Agar aap yeh click karte hain aur baad mein Madadgar apna payment/madad proof sahi tareeke se dikha deta hai, toh aapka account permanently block kar diya jaata hai. Isliye is button ka istemal poori imandaari se karein.

Step 2: Intezaar Karein

Click karne ke baad, Madadgar ko 5 din diye jaate hain apna proof dene ke liye. Aapko is beech kuch nahi karna — Seva Connect team poora process dekhti hai.

Step 3: Faisla Aane Ka Intezaar Karein

Agar Madadgar proof deta hai, Seva Connect team use dekh kar faisla leti hai. Do sthiti ho sakti hain:

  • Madadgar ka proof sahi paaya jaata hai — is sthiti mein aapka account permanently block kar diya jaata hai, kyunki aapki report galat paayi gayi. Agar aapko lagta hai yeh kisi galatfehmi ki wajah se hua hai, toh aap Contact Page se poori jaankari ke saath apni baat rakh sakte hain, aur team dobara check karegi.
  • Madadgar proof nahi deta, ya proof galat paaya jaata hai — is sthiti mein aapka case wapas Verified status mein chala jaata hai, aur naya Madadgar dhoondha jaata hai.

Agar Aap Madadgar Hain — Step By Step

Step 1: Email Ka Intezaar Karein

Agar Zaruratmand ne "Nahi Mili" report ki hai, aapko turant ek email milta hai jisme yeh bataya jaata hai, aur aapko 5 din ki samay-seema di jaati hai apna proof dene ke liye.

Zaroori chetavani: Agar aap 5 din ke andar apna proof nahi dete, ya diya gaya proof valid nahi paaya jaata, toh aapka account permanently block kar diya jaata hai. Isliye jaise hi yeh email mile, jald se jald apna proof taiyar karein.

Step 2: Dispute Page Par Jaayein

Email mein diye gaye link se Dispute Page (dispute) par jaayein. Wahan aapko yeh bharna hai:

  • Apna poora naam
  • Payment ya madad ka proof — iska Google Drive link, jo public access ke saath ho, taaki Seva Connect team use khol kar dekh sake.

Step 3: Faisla Aane Ka Intezaar Karein

Proof submit karne ke baad case ka status Proof Submitted ho jaata hai. Seva Connect team use dekhti hai, aur do sthiti ho sakti hain:

  • Aapka proof sahi paaya jaata hai — Zaruratmand ka account permanently block ho jaata hai, aur aapka case yahin khatam ho jaata hai, koi asar nahi padta.
  • Aapka proof valid nahi paaya jaata — agar 5-din ki samay-seema abhi bachi hai, aapko dobara sahi proof dene ka mauka milta hai. Agar samay-seema nikal chuki hai, aapka account permanently block kar diya jaata hai.

Yaad rakhein: agar proof samay par de diya gaya hai, toh sirf isliye block nahi kiya jaata ki review mein thoda samay lag gaya. Samay-seema ka dabaav sirf tab tak hai jab tak proof aata nahi.

Account Permanently Block Hone Ke Baad Kya Ho Sakta Hai

Chahe Zaruratmand block ho ya Madadgar — agar unhe lagta hai yeh galti se hua hai, ya koi genuine wajah thi, woh Contact Page ke zariye poori jaankari ke saath apni baat rakh sakte hain. Seva Connect team dobara verify karke faisla leti hai.

Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Kya "Nahi Mili" button dabane se Madadgar turant block ho jaata hai?

Nahi. Yeh sirf ek dispute shuru karta hai. Madadgar ko apni baat rakhne aur proof dene ka poora 5-din ka mauka milta hai.

Agar main galti se "Nahi Mili" click kar dun, toh kya hoga?

Agar Madadgar apna proof sahi tareeke se de deta hai, toh aapka account permanently block ho sakta hai. Isliye yeh button sirf tab click karein jab sach mein madad na mili ho.

Proof Google Drive link hi kyun chahiye, aur public access kyun zaroori hai?

Taaki Seva Connect team seedhe usse khol kar dekh sake. Agar link private hai, toh team use dekh nahi paayegi, aur proof invalid maana ja sakta hai.

Agar mera account permanently block ho jaaye, toh kya main dobara register kar sakta hoon?

Aapko naya account banane ki koshish nahi karni chahiye. Iske bajaye Contact Page se apni poori baat aur wajah bataayein — Seva Connect team aapke case ko dobara dekhegi.

Aage Kya

Ab tak humne registration se lekar dispute tak, poora safar dono taraf se dekh liya hai. Agla article Seva Saathi ke baare mein hai — jo log is poore system ka daayra badhate hain.
