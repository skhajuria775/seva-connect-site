Agar Madad Nahi Mili — Dispute Kaise Karein

10 min padhne mein · Trust & Safety

Bahut kam hota hai, lekin agar kabhi Madadgar ke confirmation ke baad bhi madad na mile, toh Zaruratmand ke paas apni baat rakhne ka ek saaf aur fair tareeka hai.

"Nahi Mili" — dispute shuru karna

Jab Madadgar "Maine Madad Kar Di" confirm karta hai, Zaruratmand ke email mein ek "Nahi Mili" button bhi hota hai. Agar sach mein madad nahi mili, Zaruratmand yeh button daba sakta hai.

Click hote hi case ka status DISPUTED ho jaata hai, aur Madadgar ko ek email jaata hai jisme 5 din ki deadline ke andar proof daalne ka link hota hai.

Madadgar ka pehla mauka — proof daalna

Madadgar apna proof (jaise baat-cheet ka screenshot, photo, ya jo bhi kaam ka ho) 5 din ke andar daal sakta hai. Jaise hi proof aata hai, status PROOF_SUBMITTED ho jaata hai, aur ab admin ke faisle ka intezaar hota hai.

Agar proof time par daal diya gaya hai, toh Madadgar ko sirf isliye block nahi kiya jaata ki admin ko dekhne mein thoda samay lag gaya — deadline ka dabaav sirf tab tak hai jab tak proof aata nahi.

Bich mein ek reminder bhi jaata hai

Agar 2 din ki deadline bachi ho (yaani 3-vein din tak proof nahi aaya), Madadgar ko ek reminder email jaata hai — "Sirf 2 din baaki hain, proof daal dein."

Admin ka faisla

Proof dekhne ke baad, admin do faisle le sakta hai:

  • Proof sahi hai (Genuine) — iska matlab Zaruratmand ne galat dispute kiya tha. Aisi sthiti mein Zaruratmand ka account block ho jaata hai. Lekin agar unhe lagta hai yeh galti se hua, woh Contact page se poori jaankari ke saath apni baat rakh sakte hain, aur team dobara check karegi.
  • Proof galat hai (Fake/Invalid) — agar 5-din ki deadline abhi bachi hai, Madadgar ko dobara proof bhejne ka mauka milta hai. Agar deadline nikal chuki hai, Madadgar ka account block ho jaata hai.

Agar proof kabhi aaya hi nahi

Agar Madadgar 5 din tak koi proof nahi bhejta, ek roz ka check khud-b-khud unka account block kar deta hai.

Account block hone ke baad kya hota hai

Chahe Zaruratmand block ho ya Madadgar — jis bhi taraf ka dispute sahi paaya jaaye, dusri taraf ka case wapas VERIFIED status mein chala jaata hai. Isse matching dobara shuru ho jaati hai, taaki genuine Zaruratmand ko naya Madadgar mil sake.

Yeh tareeka kyun fair hai

Har taraf ko apni baat rakhne, proof dene aur samajhne ka poora mauka milta hai — koi bhi faisla sirf ek click se nahi hota, admin har case ko dekh kar faisla karta hai.
