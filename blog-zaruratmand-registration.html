Zaruratmand Kaise Register Karein — Poora Tareeka

9 min padhne mein · Shuruwaat Karein

Agar aapko ya aapke kisi jaan-pehchaan wale ko sach mein madad chahiye, toh registration ka poora tareeka seedha aur saaf hai. Yahan har cheez ek-ek karke samjhaya gaya hai.

Registration form kahan hai

Registration form is page par hai. Form 4 chhote steps mein bata hua hai — Jaankari, Zarurat, Video, Submit — taaki bharna aasan lage.

Kaunsi jaankari zaroor bharni hai

Yeh sab fields zaroori hain, inke bina form submit hi nahi hoga:

  • Poora naam
  • Phone number — 10 digit ka, isi par Madadgar se baat hogi.
  • Email address — isi par match ki jaankari aur Shukriya-reminder aayega.
  • Pincode, shehar aur rajya (state) — isi se pata chalta hai ki aapke sabse kareeb kaun Madadgar hai.
  • Category — kis tarah ki madad chahiye (jaise khana, padhai, medical, waghera).

Document aur video — yeh optional hai

Form mein document ya video daalne ka bhi option hai, lekin yeh zaroori nahi hai — agar aap nahi daalte, tab bhi registration ho jaayega. Lekin agar aap daal dete hain, toh do fayde hote hain:

  • Aapke case ko pehle match milne ke chances badh jaate hain.
  • Agar aap chahein toh apni video Cases page aur Home page par public dikhayi ja sakti hai, taaki jaldi madad mil sake.

Duplicate aur banned check — kya hota hai

Jaise hi aap submit karte hain, system automatically kuch cheezein check karta hai:

  1. Kya yeh phone ya email pehle se block (banned) hai? Agar haan, registration nahi ho payega.
  2. Kya aapka koi case pehle se pending hai? Ek waqt mein sirf ek hi active case allowed hai.
  3. Kya aapka koi purana case complete ho chuka hai jiska abhi tak Shukriya nahi bola gaya? Agar haan, pehle usse poora karna hoga, phir naya case register hoga.
Yaad rakhein: Phone aur email dono ek saath check hote hain. Sirf ek cheez badal kar dobara register nahi kiya ja sakta.

Submit karne ke baad kya hota hai

Submit hote hi aapka case PENDING status mein chala jaata hai. Iska matlab hai ki abhi tak admin ne dekha nahi hai. Aapko ek confirmation email bhi milta hai — agar Inbox mein na dikhe toh Spam/Junk folder zaroor check karein.

Ab agla kaam admin ka hai — woh aapki poori jaankari dekhega, aur agar sab theek laga toh case ko Verify karega. Yeh poora process agle article mein samjhaya gaya hai.
