🙏 Madad Mil Gayi? Shukriya Video Bhejo

Sirf 1 minute lagega. Aapka chhota sa "Shukriya" video, Madadgar ke liye sabse bada tohfa hota hai.

Video Kaise Banayein Aur Bhejein?

3 simple steps — bilkul free, koi app download nahi karni

1

📱 30-60 second ka video banao

Apne phone ke camera open karo aur Bolo: "Mera naam ___ hai, mujhe ___ mein madad mili, bahut bahut shukriya 🙏"

2

📁 Google Drive par upload karo

Google Drive app kholo → "+" button → Upload → apni video chuniye → upload hone do.

3

🔗 Share link copy karke neeche paste karo

Video pe long-press karo → Share → "Anyone with the link" ON karo → Copy link → yahan paste karo.

📋 Yeh ID aapko registration ke time mila tha

Jitne logo ne madad ki, sab select karo (ek ho ya ek se zyada)

Madadgar list load ho rahi hai...

Is Case ID ke liye koi matched Madadgar nahi mila. Case ID sahi check karo, ya thodi der baad try karo.

📁 Drive mein video upload karke "Anyone with the link" access do, phir link yahan paste karo.

📸 Agar is madad ki koi photo hai (same "Anyone with the link" access ON karke), to yahan uska Drive link do — yeh humari Photo Gallery par dikhayi jaayegi. Optional hai, chhod bhi sakte ho.

🙈 Agar apna chehra nahi dikhana chahte, toh video banate waqt khud chehra blur ya crop kar lein — yeh platform automatically nahi karta.

🎉

Shukriya Bhej Diya Gaya!

Aapka video review ke baad Stories page par dikhaya jayega. Madadgar ko bhi bahut khushi hogi yeh dekh kar! ❤️
