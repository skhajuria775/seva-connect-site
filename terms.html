📋 Seva Connect Terms of Use

Seva Connect use karne ke niyam, responsibilities aur platform ki limitations

Last Updated: August 2026
🙏 Seva Connect ek 100% social impact platform hai. Hum sirf zaruratmand logon ko Madadgars se connect karte hain. Koi financial transaction platform ke through nahi hota.

1. Platform Ka Kaam

Seva Connect ek connecting platform hai jo genuine Zaruratmandon ko un logon se connect karta hai jo apni ichha se madad karna chahte hain. Seva Connect khud paisa collect nahi karta aur kisi direct financial transaction mein party nahi hota. Madad ka arrangement seedha Madadgar aur Zaruratmand ke beech hota hai.

2. Madadgar Ki Zimmedari

Jo log Seva Connect par Madadgar ke roop mein register karte hain, woh apni ichha se aur bina kisi pressure ke madad karte hain. Seva Connect kisi Madadgar se help karne ki guaranteed commitment nahi leta. Platform matching facilitate karta hai; kisi Zaruratmand ki madad karna ya nahi karna Madadgar ka apna faisla hai.

3. Zaruratmand Ki Zimmedari

Jo log Seva Connect par help ke liye register karte hain, unhe apni zarurat aur situation ke baare mein sahi aur genuine information deni hogi. Galat, misleading ya fraudulent information dene par account suspend ya permanently block kiya ja sakta hai. Platform ka misuse strictly prohibited hai.

4. Verification

Seva Connect cases ko available information, documents, video aur direct contact jaise sources ke zariye verify karne ki poori koshish karta hai. Lekin koi bhi verification process 100% guarantee nahi de sakta. Verification hamari best effort hai. Kisi case ke basis par help karne ka final decision Madadgar ko apni samajh aur available information ke according lena hota hai.

5. Free Service

Seva Connect par Madadgar aur Zaruratmand dono ke liye platform currently free hai. Registration aur platform use karne ke liye koi subscription ya hidden platform charge nahi hai.

6. Platform Ki Seema

Seva Connect ek connecting platform hai, financial intermediary nahi. Madadgar aur Zaruratmand ke beech hone wali direct dealings Seva Connect ke through financial transaction nahi hoti. Seva Connect kisi direct help arrangement ki amount, outcome ya timing ki guarantee nahi deta.

7. Content Policy

Seva Connect par offensive, misleading, fraudulent ya harmful content post karna prohibited hai. Platform ki safety aur community standards maintain karne ke liye Seva Connect ko aise content ko review, restrict ya remove karne ka adhikar hai.

8. Changes

Seva Connect zarurat ke anusaar in Terms of Use ko update ya revise kar sakta hai. Updated terms isi page par publish ki jayengi. Terms mein changes ke baad platform ka continued use updated terms ko review karne aur unke applicable provisions ke saath continue karne ko indicate karta hai.
