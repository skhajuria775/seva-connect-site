Hum Kya Hain

Seva Connect ek connecting platform hai — hum genuine zaruratmandon ko un logon se milate hain jo dil se madad karna chahte hain. Hum khud koi paisa collect nahi karte, koi donation process nahi karte, aur kisi bhi financial transaction mein seedha shamil nahi hote.

Hamara role sirf yeh hai: Verification → Trust → Matching → Stories.

Hum Kya Nahi Hain

  • Hum ek registered NGO, charitable trust, ya financial institution nahi hain (jab tak legal registration complete na ho).
  • Hum kisi transaction ki guarantee, escrow, ya payment processing nahi karte.
  • Hum madad ki amount, quality, ya timing ki guarantee nahi de sakte — yeh seedha Madadgar aur Zaruratmand ke beech tay hota hai.
  • Hum kisi tax-deduction ya official receipt ki facility abhi nahi dete (jab tak Trust registration na ho jaaye).

Verification Ki Limits

Hum har case ko manually verify karne ki poori koshish karte hain — video, documents, aur direct contact ke zariye. Lekin koi bhi verification process 100% fool-proof nahi ho sakta.

Important: Madadgar se hum request karte hain ki woh apni samajh aur comfort ke hisaab se hi decision le. Seva Connect kisi galat information ki wajah se hone wale nuksan ke liye legally responsible nahi hai, lekin hum har fraud case ko seriously lete hain aur turant action lete hain.

Direct Transactions Ki Responsibility

Jab ek Madadgar kisi Zaruratmand ki madad karta hai — paisa, samaan, ya koi aur tarika — yeh transaction seedha unke beech hota hai, Seva Connect ke bina kisi involvement ke. Iska matlab:

  • Madadgar apni marzi se decide karta hai kitni aur kaise madad karni hai.
  • Seva Connect is transaction ka koi record, proof, ya guarantee nahi rakhta.
  • Kisi vivad (dispute) ki situation mein, Seva Connect ek neutral connecting platform hai — hum madad ki koshish kar sakte hain lekin legal arbitrator nahi hain.

Third-Party Links Aur Content

Hamari website par YouTube videos, social media links, aur external resources ho sakte hain. Hum un platforms ki content, privacy practices, ya availability ke liye responsible nahi hain.

Disclaimer Mein Changes

Hum yeh disclaimer samay samay par update kar sakte hain jaise platform grow karta hai (jaise legal registration ke baad). Latest version hamesha is page par milegi.
