📋 Disclaimer

Seva Connect use karne se pehle, kripya yeh disclaimer dhyan se padhiye. Is page par hum Seva Connect ka role, verification ki limits, direct help transactions ki responsibility aur platform ki important limitations ko clearly explain karte hain.

Hum Kya Hain

Seva Connect ek connecting platform jo genuine zaruratmandon ko un logon se connect karta hai jo apni ichha se madad karna chahte hain. Seva Connect khud paisa collect nahi karta, donation process operate nahi karta aur kisi direct financial transaction mein party nahi hota. Hamara role platform par available information ke basis par verification, trust-building, matching aur genuine help connections ko facilitate karna hai.

Hamara role sirf yeh hai: Verification → Trust → Matching → Stories.

Hum Kya Nahi Hain

  • Seva Connect ek connecting platform hai. Yeh platform khud kisi direct financial transaction ka party nahi hai.
  • • Seva Connect kisi transaction ki guarantee, escrow, ya payment processing nahi karta.
  • • Madad ki amount, quality aur timing ki koi guarantee nahi di ja sakti — yeh Madadgar aur Zaruratmand ke beech tay hoti hai.
  • • Seva Connect abhi tax-deduction ya official donation receipt provide nahi karta.

Verification Ki Limits

Hum har case ko manually verify karne ki poori koshish karte hain — video, documents aur direct contact jaise available sources ke zariye. Lekin koi bhi verification process 100% fool-proof nahi ho sakta.

Important: Isliye Madadgar ko kisi bhi help ka decision apni samajh, comfort aur available information ke basis par lena chahiye. Agar kisi case mein galat ya misleading information milti hai, to Seva Connect use seriously leta hai aur available process ke according action karne ki koshish karta hai.

Direct Transactions Ki Responsibility

Jab koi Madadgar kisi Zaruratmand ki madad karta hai — paisa, samaan ya kisi aur tarike se — to yeh arrangement seedha Madadgar aur Zaruratmand ke beech hota hai. Seva Connect us direct transaction ka party nahi hota.Iska matlab:

  • • Madadgar apni marzi se decide karta hai ki kitni aur kis tarah ki madad karni hai.
  • • Seva Connect direct transaction ki guarantee nahi deta.
  • • Kisi dispute ki situation mein Seva Connect ek neutral connecting platform ki role mein rehta hai aur legal arbitrator nahi hai.

Third-Party Links Aur Content

Hamari website par YouTube videos, social media links aur other external resources ho sakte hain. In third-party platforms ki content, privacy practices, terms ya availability Seva Connect ke control mein nahi hoti. Kisi external platform ko use karne se pehle uski applicable policies aur terms ko review karein.

Disclaimer Mein Changes

Seva Connect zarurat ke anusaar is disclaimer ko update kar sakta hai. Kisi bhi update ke baad latest version isi page par available hogi. Page par di gayi "Last Updated" date se aap latest update ko identify kar sakte hain.

Sawal Hai?
