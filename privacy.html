🔒 Privacy Policy

Aapka data safe hai — hamara waada

Last Updated: June 2026
✅ Seva Connect aapka data kabhi bhi kisi third party ko nahi bechta aur na hi share karta hai. Sirf matching ke liye use hota hai.

1. Hum Kya Data Collect Karte Hain?

Madadgar registration se:

  • Naam, Phone number, Email (optional)
  • City, State, PIN code
  • Help categories aur capacity
  • Optional motivation video link

Zaruratmand registration se:

  • Naam, Phone number
  • City, State, PIN code
  • Zarurat ki category aur story
  • Optional document links aur video

2. Data Ka Use Kaise Hota Hai?

  • Madadgar aur Zaruratmand ka smart match dhundhne ke liye
  • Admin verification process ke liye
  • Match notification email/WhatsApp bhejne ke liye
  • Platform improvement ke liye (anonymized stats)

3. Privacy Protection

  • Zaruratmand ka poora address public nahi hota — sirf city
  • Phone number sirf match ke time Madadgar ko diya jaata hai
  • Bachon ki photos blur ki jaati hain
  • Data Google Sheets mein secure store hota hai

4. Third Party Sharing

Aapka personal data (naam, phone, city, story) kisi third party ko nahi becha jaata, na rent pe diya jaata hai. Google Analytics sirf anonymized traffic data dekhta hai. Advertising ke liye Google aur uske partners cookies use kar sakte hain — iski details Section 5 mein hai.

5. Cookies Aur Advertising

Hum Google Analytics ke liye basic cookies use karte hain jo sirf website traffic count karte hain. Koi personal data track nahi hota.

Third-party vendors, jisme Google shamil hai, cookies (jaise Google ki DoubleClick cookie) use karke ads dikhate hain jo aapki pichli visits (is site ya doosri sites pe) ke basis pe hote hain. Google Analytics ke saath advertising-features (jaise Display Advertising) bhi use ho sakte hain, jisse demographic aur interest-reporting milti hai.

Aap Google ki advertising-cookie ke use ko opt-out kar sakte hain Google Ads Settings pe jaake, ya aboutads.info pe jaake third-party vendor cookies opt-out kar sakte hain.

6. Data Delete Karna

Aap apna data delete karwana chahte hain? Email karo: helpsevaconnect@gmail.com
Hum 7 working days mein aapka data delete kar denge.

7. Data Security

Aapka data Google ke secure servers pe store hota hai. Hum regular basis pe access review karte hain. Sirf admin ko data access hota hai.
