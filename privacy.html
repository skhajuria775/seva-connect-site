🔒 Seva Connect Privacy Policy

Aapka personal data kaise collect, use aur protect kiya jaata hai — yeh Privacy Policy uski jaankari deti hai.

Last Updated: August 2026
✅ Seva Connect aapke data ka use platform ke matching, verification, communication aur improvement jaise stated purposes ke liye karta hai. Is page par hum batate hain ki kaunsa data collect kiya jaata hai, kaise use hota hai aur cookies aur third-party services ka kya role hai.

1. Hum Kya Data Collect Karte Hain?

Madadgar registration se:

  • Naam, Phone number, Email
  • City, State, PIN code
  • Help categories aur capacity
  • Optional motivation video link

Zaruratmand registration se:

  • Naam, Phone number
  • City, State, PIN code
  • Zarurat ki category aur story
  • Document links aur video

2. Data Ka Use Kaise Hota Hai?

  • Madadgar aur Zaruratmand ke beech suitable match dhundhne ke liye
  • Admin verification process ke liye
  • Match notification email ya WhatsApp ke zariye bhejne ke liye
  • Platform improvement ke liye anonymized statistics ke saath

3. Privacy Protection

  • Zaruratmand ka poora address public nahi hota — public information mein sirf city dikhayi jaati hai.
  • Phone number matching ke time relevant Madadgar ko diya jaata hai.
  • Registration aur matching se related data Cloud Firestore mein store kiya jaata hai.

4. Third Party Sharing

Aapka personal data — jaise naam, phone number, city aur story — third parties ko becha ya rent par nahi diya jaata. Website analytics ke liye Google Analytics ka use kiya ja sakta hai, jahan anonymized traffic information process ho sakti hai. Advertising ke liye Google aur uske partners cookies ka use kar sakte hain. Iske baare mein details next section mein di gayi hain.

5. Cookies Aur Advertising

Seva Connect website traffic aur usage ko samajhne ke liye Google Analytics jaise analytics tools ka use kar sakta hai. In tools ke through cookies aur related technologies use ho sakti hain.

Third-party vendors, including Google, advertising ke liye cookies use kar sakte hain. In cookies ka use previous visits aur browsing activity ke basis par relevant ads dikhane aur advertising-related reporting ke liye kiya ja sakta hai.

Aap Google ki advertising cookies ko Google Ads Settings ke through manage ya opt out kar sakte hain. Third-party vendor cookies ke liye available opt-out options bhi use kiye ja sakte hain.

6. Data Delete Karna

Aap apna data delete karwana chahte hain? Email karo: helpsevaconnect@gmail.com
Hum 7 working days mein aapka data delete kar denge.

7. Data Security

Aapka data Google ke secure servers pe store hota hai. Hum regular basis pe access review karte hain. Poora database sirf admin ko access hota hai — matching hone par sirf zaroori contact details (jaise phone number) us Madadgar ko diye jaate hain jiske saath match hua ho, taaki madad coordinate ho sake.
