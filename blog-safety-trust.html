Fraud Kaise Roka Jaata Hai — Safety System

8 min padhne mein · Trust & Safety

Jab kaam hi logon ki zaroorat aur bharose se juda ho, safety koi extra cheez nahi hoti — woh sabse zaroori buniyad hoti hai. Yahan hai woh sab kuch jo Seva Connect ko genuine banaye rakhta hai.

Har case ek insaan dekhta hai

Koi bhi registration khud-b-khud pass (approve) nahi hoti. Har Zaruratmand ka case, har video, har dispute — admin khud dekhta hai. Yeh jaan-boojh kar rakha gaya hai, taaki koi galat case system se seedha na nikal jaaye.

Duplicate aur banned-check

Registration ke waqt hi system phone aur email dono check karta hai — agar koi pehle se block (banned) hai, dobara registration nahi ho sakta. Yeh check Zaruratmand aur Madadgar, dono taraf se hota hai.

Dispute se ban hone ka system

Jaisa humne pichle article mein dekha, agar dispute mein koi galat paaya jaata hai (chahe Zaruratmand ho ya Madadgar), unka account block ho jaata hai. Yeh system dono taraf se fair hai — sirf ilzaam lagane se koi block nahi hota, proof aur admin ke dekhne ke baad hi faisla hota hai.

Unban — galti sudharne ka mauka

Agar kabhi galti se koi block ho jaaye, admin manually unban kar sakta hai. Yeh hamesha ek socha-samjha faisla hota hai — koi apne-aap unban nahi hota.

Sabse bada bharosa — paisa kabhi platform se nahi guzarta

Yeh baat dobara bolna zaroori hai: Seva Connect kabhi kisi ka paisa apne paas nahi rakhta. Madadgar seedhe Zaruratmand tak madad pahunchate hain. Isse dhoka-dhadi ki sambhavna hi khatam ho jaati hai — kyunki platform kabhi bhi paisa apne haath mein leta hi nahi.

Safety ka matlab sirf rules nahi hai — iska matlab hai ek system jo har kadam par insaani samajh ko computer ke saath jodta hai.

Yeh sab kyun zaroori hai

Ek platform jo logon ko unki sabse badi zaroorat ke waqt jodta hai, uski sabse badi zimmedari hai ki woh bharosemand rahe. Yehi wajah hai ki har cheez — verification se lekar dispute tak — isi ek uddeshya ke saath banayi gayi hai.
