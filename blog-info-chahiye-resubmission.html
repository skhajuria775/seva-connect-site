Agar Document Ya Video Poora Nahi Hai — Info Chahiye Path

6 min padhne mein · Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Kabhi-kabhi jo jaankari di gayi hai woh poori tarah spasht nahi hoti. Aisi sthiti mein case reject nahi kiya jaata — ek aur mauka diya jaata hai.

Jab admin ko aur jaankari chahiye hoti hai

Agar admin ko lagta hai ki diya gaya document ya video Zaruratmand ki zaroorat ko poori tarah spasht nahi karta, toh case reject nahi hota. Iske bajaye, admin ek wajah likh kar case ko "Info Chahiye" mark karta hai. Case ka status INFO_NEEDED ho jaata hai.

Zaruratmand ko kya milta hai

Turant ek email jaata hai jisme:

  • Yeh saaf likha hota hai ki case reject nahi hua — woh abhi bhi active hai.
  • Wajah batayi jaati hai ki kya aur jaankari chahiye.
  • Ek link hota hai jahan se naya document ya video daala ja sakta hai.

Naya document/video kaise daalein

Email mein diye gaye link par jaake, Zaruratmand naya document link ya naya video link daal sakta hai. Kam se kam ek cheez daalni zaroori hai — ya toh document, ya video.

Resubmit karne ke baad kya hota hai

Jaise hi naya document/video submit hota hai, case ka status wapas PENDING ho jaata hai. Iska matlab hai ki case admin ke saamne dobara review ke liye aa gaya hai. Admin phir se dekhega, aur do faisle le sakta hai:

  • Verify Karo — agar ab sab kuch saaf hai, aur case aage badh jaata hai (verification/matching wala process shuru ho jaata hai).
  • Dobara Info Chahiye — agar abhi bhi kuch missing hai.
Zaroori baat: Is poore process mein case kabhi delete nahi hota. Jab tak Zaruratmand khud jawab nahi deta, case system mein bana rehta hai — taaki genuine zaroorat kabhi chhoot na jaaye.

Yeh tareeka kyun rakha gaya hai

Sirf "haan" ya "na" bolne ke bajaye, ek fair aage-peeche wala process rakha gaya hai, taaki galti se koi genuine case reject na ho jaaye sirf isliye ki pehli baar mein kuch chhoot gaya tha.
