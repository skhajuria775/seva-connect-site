Agar Document Ya Video Poora Nahi Hai — Complete Information

7 min padhne mein · Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Har case pehli hi koshish mein poora saaf nahi hota — aur yeh koi badi baat nahi hai. Seva Connect ne isi ke liye ek fair, dobara-mauka wala tareeka banaya hai.

Jab Pehli Koshish Poori Tarah Kaam Na Aaye

Har baar aisa nahi hota ki pehli hi koshish mein sab kuch bilkul saaf ho jaaye. Kabhi document adhoora hota hai, kabhi video mein zaroori jaankari nahi dikhti. Aisi sthiti mein Seva Connect case ko turant reject nahi karta — balki ek aur mauka deta hai. Isi ko Info Chahiye path kaha jaata hai.

Jab Seva Connect Team Ko Aur Jaankari Chahiye Hoti Hai

Jab Seva Connect team kisi case ko dekhti hai aur use lagta hai ki diya gaya document ya video zaroorat ko poori tarah spasht nahi karta, toh case reject nahi kiya jaata. Iske bajaye, case ko Info Chahiye status mein daal diya jaata hai, aur ek saaf wajah likhi jaati hai.

Info Chahiye Kin Wajahon Se Aata Hai

Verification team jo cheezein dekhti hai unhi ke hisaab se "Info Chahiye" ki wajah bhi tay hoti hai. Kuch sabse common reasons yeh hote hain:

  • Video mein zaroorat clearly nahi dikhti — jaise sirf general baat ki gayi ho, lekin exact zaroorat (kitna amount, kis cheez ke liye) na bataya gaya ho.
  • Document blurry ya adhoora hai — jaise fee receipt ka sirf ek hissa dikhta ho, ya medical report ka page missing ho.
  • Story aur document match nahi karte — jaise registration mein di gayi jaankari aur document mein likhi detail alag ho.
  • Phone number contact nahi ho pa raha — agar team confirm karne ke liye call karti hai aur number switch-off ya galat milta hai.
  • Amount justify nahi ho pa raha — jab mangi gayi rakam aur diya gaya document/zaroorat aapas mein match nahi karte.

Yeh wahi cheezein hain jo Verification Policy mein bataya gaya hai ki team har case mein check karti hai — isliye pehli koshish mein hi inhe dhyan mein rakhna resubmission ki zaroorat kaafi kam kar deta hai.

Pehli Hi Koshish Mein Sahi Kaise Karein

Info Chahiye se bachne ke kuch simple tareeke hain:

  • Video record karte waqt saaf awaaz mein bataayein — kya zaroorat hai, kitna amount chahiye, aur kis liye.
  • Document ki photo achi roshni mein lein, taaki poora text saaf padha ja sake — koi bhi corner cut na ho.
  • Google Drive link share karne se pehle sharing setting "Anyone with the link" par set karein — agar setting "Restricted" rahi, toh team file open hi nahi kar paayegi aur link invalid dikhega.
  • Registration form mein di gayi detail (naam, city, amount) aur document/video mein di gayi detail same rakhein.

Zaruratmand Ko Kya Milta Hai

Turant ek email jaata hai, jisme:

  • Yeh saaf likha hota hai ki case abhi bhi active hai — reject nahi hua hai.
  • Wajah batayi jaati hai — Seva Connect team ne kya bataya hai ki kya aur chahiye.
  • Resubmit Page ka link diya jaata hai — jahan se naya document ya video daala ja sakta hai.

Resubmit Page Par Kya Karna Hai

Email mein diye gaye link se aap Resubmit Page (resubmit) par pahunchte hain. Yahan do fields hoti hain:

  • Naya Document Ka Google Drive Link
  • Naya Video Ka Google Drive Link

In dono mein se kam se kam ek zaroor bharni hai — ya toh document ka link, ya video ka link, taaki case aage badh sake. Agar dono khaali chhode gaye, toh form submit nahi hoga.

Yeh page sirf tab kaam karta hai jab aapka case Info Chahiye status mein ho. Agar case pehle se hi aage badh chuka hai, toh yeh page dobara istemal nahi kiya ja sakta.

Agar Email Nahi Mil Raha

Kai baar "Info Chahiye" ki email spam ya promotions folder mein chali jaati hai, especially agar aapne pehli baar Seva Connect se email receive ki ho. Agar aapne apna case register kiya hai aur kuch dinon se koi update nahi mila, toh sabse pehle spam/junk folder check karein — email helpsevaconnect@gmail.com se aati hai. Agar wahan bhi nahi mile, seedha Contact Page se WhatsApp ya email par apna registered phone number ya naam bata kar status poochh sakte hain.

Resubmit Karne Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai

Jaise hi naya document ya video daala jaata hai, case ka status wapas Pending ho jaata hai. Iska matlab hai ki case Seva Connect team ke saamne dobara review ke liye aa gaya hai — yeh dobara review bhi generally 24 se 48 ghante ke andar hi complete ho jaata hai, jaisa har naye case ke saath hota hai. Team phir se poori jaankari dekhti hai, aur do faisle ho sakte hain:

  • Case Verify ho jaata hai — agar ab sab kuch saaf hai, aur pichle article mein bataya gaya matching-process shuru ho jaata hai.
  • Dobara Info Chahiye — agar abhi bhi kuch missing hai.
Zaroori baat: is poore process mein case kabhi delete nahi hota. Jab tak Zaruratmand khud jawab nahi deta, case system mein bana rehta hai.

"Info Chahiye", Reject Aur Dispute Mein Farak

Teeno alag situations hain, aur inhe alag samajhna zaroori hai:

  • Info Chahiye — verification se pehle hota hai, jab submit ki gayi jaankari poori tarah spasht nahi hoti. Case active rehta hai, sirf thodi aur detail chahiye hoti hai.
  • Reject — sirf tab hota hai jab verification ke dauraan ya baad mein galat/misleading information milti hai (jaisa Verification Policy mein bataya gaya hai). Genuine Zaruratmand ka case, jise sirf thodi aur clarity chahiye, reject nahi kiya jaata.
  • Dispute — yeh ek bilkul alag stage hai jo verification ke baad aata hai, jab madad confirm ho chuki ho lekin Zaruratmand tak sach mein na pahunchi ho. Iska poora tareeka agle article mein hai.

Yeh farak samajhna isliye zaroori hai taaki agar aapko "Info Chahiye" mile, aap ghabraein nahi — yeh na reject hai, na koi dispute, sirf ek chhota sa aur kadam hai.

Ek Example Se Samjhein

Maan lijiye ek Zaruratmand ne medical zaroorat ke liye register kiya, lekin submit kiya gaya document sirf hospital ka bill dikhata hai, patient ka naam ya diagnosis clearly nahi dikhta. Team is case ko "Info Chahiye" mein daal degi aur email mein likhegi ki "diagnosis wala page ya doctor ka prescription bhi chahiye". Zaruratmand Resubmit Page par jaakar sirf ek naya Drive link daal dega jisme woh missing page ho — poora form dobara bharne ki zaroorat nahi. Case turant wapas review mein chala jaata hai, aur agar ab sab clear hai, 24-48 ghante mein verify ho jaata hai.

Yeh Fair System Kyun Zaroori Hai

Bahut si genuine zarooratein sirf isliye reject ho jaati hain kyunki pehli koshish mein koi chhoti si cheez chhoot jaati hai — khaas kar un logon ke liye jo pehli baar aisa form bhar rahe hain ya jinke paas documents scan karne ki achi facility nahi hai. Agar Seva Connect aise cases ko turant reject kar de, toh kai genuine zarooratmand sirf ek chhoti si galti ki wajah se madad se vanchit reh jaayenge. Isi wajah se "Info Chahiye" jaisa dobara-mauka wala system zaroori hai — taaki platform strict bhi rahe aur fair bhi.

Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Kya "Info Chahiye" milna ek buri baat hai?

Nahi. Yeh sirf ek sanket hai ki thoda aur jaankari chahiye, taaki aapki zaroorat sahi tareeke se samjhi ja sake.

Kitni baar resubmit kiya ja sakta hai?

Jitni baar zaroorat ho. Case kabhi bina wajah band nahi kiya jaata.

Agar mujhe samajh na aaye ki kya jaankari chahiye, toh kya karein?

Contact Page ke zariye seedhe poochha ja sakta hai.

Kya document aur video dono dena zaroori hai?

Nahi, in dono mein se sirf ek zaroori hai. Dono dena bhi thik hai, lekin sirf ek hi kaafi hai case ko aage badhane ke liye.

Resubmit karne ke baad review mein kitna time lagta hai?

Resubmission bhi ek normal review ki tarah treat hota hai, jo generally 24 se 48 ghante ke andar dekha jaata hai.

Google Drive link "access denied" ya "invalid" kyun dikhata hai?

Aksar aisa tab hota hai jab Drive file ki sharing setting restricted hoti hai. Link dene se pehle "Anyone with the link" access zaroor set karein, warna Seva Connect team file open nahi kar paayegi.

Aage Kya
