Agar Document Ya Video Poora Nahi Hai — Info Chahiye Path

7 min padhne mein · Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Har case pehli hi koshish mein poora saaf nahi hota — aur yeh koi badi baat nahi hai. Seva Connect ne isi ke liye ek fair, dobara-mauka wala tareeka banaya hai.

Jab Pehli Koshish Poori Tarah Kaam Na Aaye

Har baar aisa nahi hota ki pehli hi koshish mein sab kuch bilkul saaf ho jaaye. Kabhi document adhoora hota hai, kabhi video mein zaroori jaankari nahi dikhti. Aisi sthiti mein Seva Connect case ko turant reject nahi karta — balki ek aur mauka deta hai. Isi ko Info Chahiye path kaha jaata hai.

Jab Seva Connect Team Ko Aur Jaankari Chahiye Hoti Hai

Jab Seva Connect team kisi case ko dekhti hai aur use lagta hai ki diya gaya document ya video zaroorat ko poori tarah spasht nahi karta, toh case reject nahi kiya jaata. Iske bajaye, case ko Info Chahiye status mein daal diya jaata hai, aur ek saaf wajah likhi jaati hai.

Zaruratmand Ko Kya Milta Hai

Turant ek email jaata hai, jisme:

  • Yeh saaf likha hota hai ki case abhi bhi active hai — reject nahi hua hai.
  • Wajah batayi jaati hai — Seva Connect team ne kya bataya hai ki kya aur chahiye.
  • Resubmit Page ka link diya jaata hai — jahan se naya document ya video daala ja sakta hai.

Resubmit Page Par Kya Karna Hai

Email mein diye gaye link se aap Resubmit Page (resubmit.html) par pahunchte hain. Yahan do fields hoti hain:

  • Naya Document Ka Google Drive Link
  • Naya Video Ka Google Drive Link

In dono mein se kam se kam ek zaroor bharni hai — ya toh document ka link, ya video ka link, taaki case aage badh sake. Agar dono khaali chhode gaye, toh form submit nahi hoga.

Yeh page sirf tab kaam karta hai jab aapka case Info Chahiye status mein ho. Agar case pehle se hi aage badh chuka hai, toh yeh page dobara istemal nahi kiya ja sakta.

Resubmit Karne Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai

Jaise hi naya document ya video daala jaata hai, case ka status wapas Pending ho jaata hai. Iska matlab hai ki case Seva Connect team ke saamne dobara review ke liye aa gaya hai. Team phir se poori jaankari dekhti hai, aur do faisle ho sakte hain:

  • Case Verify ho jaata hai — agar ab sab kuch saaf hai, aur pichle article mein bataya gaya matching-process shuru ho jaata hai.
  • Dobara Info Chahiye — agar abhi bhi kuch missing hai.
Zaroori baat: is poore process mein case kabhi delete nahi hota. Jab tak Zaruratmand khud jawab nahi deta, case system mein bana rehta hai.

Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawaal

Kya "Info Chahiye" milna ek buri baat hai?

Nahi. Yeh sirf ek sanket hai ki thoda aur jaankari chahiye, taaki aapki zaroorat sahi tareeke se samjhi ja sake.

Kitni baar resubmit kiya ja sakta hai?

Jitni baar zaroorat ho. Case kabhi bina wajah band nahi kiya jaata.

Agar mujhe samajh na aaye ki kya jaankari chahiye, toh kya karein?

Contact Page ke zariye seedhe poochha ja sakta hai.

Kya document aur video dono dena zaroori hai?

Nahi, in dono mein se sirf ek zaroori hai. Dono dena bhi thik hai, lekin sirf ek hi kaafi hai case ko aage badhane ke liye.

Aage Kya

Ab ek aur zaroori sthiti dekhte hain — agar madad confirm hone ke baad bhi Zaruratmand tak sach mein na pahunche, toh kya hota hai. Yeh poora, fair dispute-resolution ka tareeka agle article mein.
